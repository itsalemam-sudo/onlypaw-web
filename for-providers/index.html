Frequently asked questions

How much can I earn as a pet care provider?

Full-time dog walkers on OnlyPaw typically earn USD 2,000–5,000 per month. Boarders with a spare bedroom often make USD 800–3,500 monthly. Pet taxi drivers set their own per-km rate. You keep 70–80% of every booking. See detailed earnings guide →

Is it free to sign up?

Yes. Creating your account, listing services, and getting verified is 100% free. You only pay when a booking is completed and you've been paid.

Which countries is OnlyPaw available in?

30+ countries — UAE, UK, USA, Canada, Australia, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman, Singapore, Hong Kong, Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon, France, Germany, Spain, Italy, Portugal, Netherlands, Ireland, New Zealand, South Africa, Turkey, Mexico, Brazil, and more. Pick your country at signup.

What do I need to become a provider?

18 or older, a valid government ID, a selfie for verification, and a passing score on the service-specific knowledge test. Some services (Pet Taxi, Boarding) need extra documents. Most applications are approved within 48 hours.

How and when do I get paid?

Escrow releases the moment the booking is complete. Payouts to your local bank are weekly, or on-demand once you hit the minimum threshold for your country.

Do I need my own insurance?

Every completed booking is covered by OnlyPaw's platform incident protection. If you already carry your own pet care liability insurance you can add proof to your profile as an extra trust signal.

Ready to earn?

Free to join. Approval in under 48 hours. First booking usually within a week.
