How to become a dog walker on OnlyPaw

  1. Sign up free. Download the OnlyPaw app or use the web form. Add your name, city, and phone.
  2. Verify identity. Upload a government ID and a live selfie. Pass a short quiz on dog body language, safe walking, harness use, weather, and emergencies.
  3. Build your profile. Add photos of you with dogs, describe your experience (past pets, breeds walked, first-aid training). Set your service radius on the map.
  4. Set your pricing. Per-walk, per-duration, or per-pet — your choice. Change any time.
  5. Publish. Once approved (usually within 48 hours), your profile appears in local search. Customers request bookings. You accept, walk, get paid.

Do you need experience?

No formal certification is required. What matters is that you understand dog body language, know when to abort a walk (heat, aggression, injury), and can handle basic emergencies. The knowledge quiz covers exactly that — most applicants pass on the first attempt. Prior dog ownership, volunteering at a shelter, or first-aid certification all help your profile stand out.

Tips to get your first bookings faster

  • Add 3–5 high-quality profile photos. You with a dog beats a headshot alone. No filters.
  • Write a real bio. Mention specific breeds you've walked, why you love dogs, and any relevant skills (first aid, reactivity work).
  • Start with a competitive price for your first 5 walks — build reviews, then raise your rate.
  • Reply within 15 minutes. Fast responders convert 3× more bookings.
  • Turn on Job Broadcast alerts so you catch one-off walk requests before other walkers.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a dog walker make on OnlyPaw?

Part-time walkers (3–5 walks/day) earn USD 900–2,400/month. Full-time walkers (6–10 walks/day, group walks) earn USD 2,500–7,000/month depending on city.

Do I need my own insurance?

Every completed walk is covered by OnlyPaw's platform incident protection. Add your own liability policy to your profile as an extra trust signal.

Can I walk in multiple neighbourhoods?

Yes — set your service radius as wide as you're willing to travel. Some walkers list two service areas (home + work).

How fast can I start earning?

Verification typically takes 24–48 hours. Most new walkers get their first booking within 3–7 days of approval, faster if they respond quickly to Job Broadcasts.
