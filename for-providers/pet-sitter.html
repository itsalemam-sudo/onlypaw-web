Two ways to sit

🔑

Drop-in visits

15–60 minute visits to the pet owner's home while they're away. Feed, walk, play, photo update. Best for owners on short trips or long workdays.

🌙

Overnight stays

Stay at the pet owner's home. Sleep there, morning and evening care, company for the pet. Higher pay, longer commitment.

How to become a pet sitter on OnlyPaw

  1. Apply free. Signup takes 3 minutes.
  2. Verify identity. Government ID + selfie. Pass the pet-care quiz (feeding, medication, emergencies, species-specific care).
  3. Build your profile. Add photos of you with animals, describe your experience, list any qualifications (vet nurse, dog trainer, etc.).
  4. Set services. Pick drop-ins, overnights, or both. Choose species and sizes. Set your rate per visit and per night.
  5. Set your radius. How far you're willing to travel to a client's home. Wider = more bookings.
  6. Publish. Approved in 24–48 hours. First booking usually within a week.

Tips to book more sits

  • Offer both drop-ins AND overnights — you'll show up in twice as many searches.
  • Say yes to cats. Cat sitters are in high demand and less competitive than dog-only sitters.
  • Send a photo update every visit. Repeat bookings from happy owners are your biggest income source.
  • Enable Job Broadcast alerts. Last-minute pet emergencies (owner stuck abroad, plans changed) usually pay premium rates.
  • Reply within 15 minutes. Response speed is a ranking factor and a booking-conversion factor.

FAQs

Can I sit for multiple households at once?

Drop-ins yes — many sitters do 5–8 short visits per day across different homes. Overnight stays are usually one household at a time.

What if a pet has medication or special needs?

Owners disclose this in the booking request. You choose whether to accept. Special-needs care usually pays a higher rate.

Am I covered if something goes wrong?

Every booking includes OnlyPaw's platform incident protection. Emergency vet costs are also covered up to a per-booking limit — you can act fast without worrying about the bill.
