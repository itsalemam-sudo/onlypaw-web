The short answer

If you live outside the US or Canada, OnlyPaw is the clear choice — Rover barely operates in most of the world. If you're in the US, the math is simple: OnlyPaw takes 20% below USD 150 vs Rover's 20% service fee + a customer-side booking fee that indirectly pushes your effective take-home lower. On a USD 40 walk, you keep USD 32 on OnlyPaw vs USD 28–30 on Rover. Multiply by 200 bookings a month.

Where OnlyPaw wins

  • Truly global. Live in Dubai, Riyadh, London, Singapore, Sydney, or any of 30+ countries? OnlyPaw was built worldwide-first. Rover barely exists outside North America.
  • Job broadcast marketplace. Customers post one-off requests (a walk today, a Sunday sit). You get pinged and quote back. Fresh bookings without waiting for organic search.
  • Response-time ranking. If you reply fast, you rank first. Rover's algorithm is more opaque and history-weighted.
  • Faster approval. 24–48 hours vs Rover's 3–10 business days.
  • Simple fee structure. One transparent tier. No booking fee hiding on the customer side that dampens your competitiveness.

Where Rover wins

  • Brand recognition in the US. Rover is a household name in the US; OnlyPaw is newer there. If you're building from scratch in Manhattan, you may still get more traffic on Rover on day one.
  • Longer track record. Rover has been around since 2011; OnlyPaw launched with worldwide-first architecture in the mid-2020s.

Can I use both?

Absolutely. Most experienced providers list on multiple platforms and take whichever bookings come in first. OnlyPaw doesn't require exclusivity — cross-list on Rover, Wag, Pawshake, and any local platform. Just make sure your calendar syncs so you don't double-book.

Bottom line

If you're outside the US, OnlyPaw is a no-brainer — Rover simply isn't available or is thinly staffed. Inside the US, OnlyPaw is the smart complement to (or replacement for) Rover: lower effective fees, faster payouts, job broadcast marketplace, and response-time ranking that rewards hustle over history.
