Requirements to become a boarder

  • 18 or older with government ID + selfie verification
  • Home photos of the room / area the pet will stay in, indoor + outdoor
  • Tenancy proof if you rent — a lease copy or landlord letter allowing pets
  • Pass the boarding knowledge quiz — feeding schedules, common medications, breed compatibility, emergency procedures
  • A safe, pet-appropriate space: no open balconies for small dogs, secure doors, a separate area for cats if hosting both

How to become a pet boarder

  1. Sign up free. Add name, city, phone.
  2. Upload documents. ID + selfie + home photos + tenancy proof (renters only).
  3. Set your capacity. Max concurrent pets, species allowed, size limits, and any breed restrictions.
  4. Set your rate + house rules. Nightly price, drop-off/pick-up windows, cancellation policy.
  5. Publish. Approval usually within 48 hours. First booking often within 1–2 weeks.

Tips to maximise occupancy

  • Say yes to cats. Cat boarding is chronically undersupplied — you'll get more bookings and fewer competitors.
  • Add 8+ photos. The room, the yard/outdoor space, food/water setup, any toys. Boarders with 8+ photos convert 2× better.
  • Enable multi-pet discounts. A 15% discount for 2 pets from the same household doubles your booking value.
  • Send twice-daily photo updates. Owners on vacation obsess over these — they drive 5-star reviews and repeat bookings.
  • List holiday availability early. Christmas, Eid, Chinese New Year, Thanksgiving — booked months in advance at premium rates.

FAQs

Do I need a fenced yard?

Big plus but not required. Apartment boarders are welcome — you'll rank higher for cats and small breeds.

Can I board multiple pets at the same time?

Yes. You set your maximum concurrent guests. Many boarders host 2–4 pets to multiply nightly income.

What if the pet damages something?

OnlyPaw's platform incident protection covers reasonable damage during a booking. We also mediate disputes on your behalf.

Can I refuse a booking?

Always. You see the pet's profile, owner's notes, and dates before deciding. Accept only what you're comfortable with — no penalties for declining.
