Platform fee, plain and simple

OnlyPaw takes 20% on bookings under USD 150, 30% on bookings USD 150 and above.

No booking fee. No listing fee. No monthly subscription. You only pay when you get paid. Every completed booking includes platform insurance and payment processing.

Compare: Rover takes 20–25% plus booking fees. Wag takes ~40%. Pawshake takes 15% plus their own booking fee that pushes total closer to 25%. OnlyPaw's flat tier structure keeps more money in your pocket, especially on standard-priced bookings.

USD 800–5,500/month depending on occupancy. Highest per-booking revenue. Providers with a fenced yard and multi-pet capacity book fastest. Holiday periods (Christmas, Eid, Chinese New Year) command 30–50% premiums. Full pet boarder earnings breakdown →

🚗 Pet Taxi

USD 1,200–3,500/month part-time (2–4 hours/day). Base rate + per-km + surcharge for large breeds. Vet clinic partnerships and grooming salon runs are consistent income.

✂️ Grooming

USD 2,500–8,000/month for mobile groomers with a converted vehicle. Salon-based groomers on OnlyPaw supplement existing shop income by USD 800–2,500 through platform bookings.

🎓 Training

Frequently asked questions

When do I get paid?

Weekly bank transfer to your local account, or on-demand once you reach your country's minimum threshold (typically USD 50 equivalent).

Do I pay taxes on OnlyPaw income?

Yes — your OnlyPaw income is treated as self-employment or freelance income in most jurisdictions. Download annual earnings reports directly from the Earnings tab in the app. Consult a local accountant for country-specific advice.

Can I offer multiple services?

Yes. Most top earners on OnlyPaw offer 2–3 services (e.g. walking + drop-in visits, boarding + grooming). Each service has its own price and knowledge quiz.

Is OnlyPaw income stable?

Pet care is remarkably recession-resistant — people pay for their pets even in downturns. Boarding follows travel seasonality (spikes at holidays); walking and drop-ins are steady year-round.
