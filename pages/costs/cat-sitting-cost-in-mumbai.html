How much does cat sitting cost in Mumbai?

2026 typical range for Mumbai, India · Prepared by OnlyPaw · Updated 2026-09-08

Cat Sitting in Mumbai: ₹500–₹1,180 per visit. This is the typical 2026 market range for the low cost tier that Mumbai sits in. Your actual booking price depends on duration, frequency, and pet-specific needs.

What drives cat sitting prices up or down in Mumbai

  • Multi-pet households — two dogs, or a dog plus a cat visit stacked in one trip — carry a per-additional-pet fee almost everywhere in Mumbai.
  • Neighborhoods with limited parking or gated access add setup time per visit, which shows up in Mumbai rates.
  • Peak season demand (Apr, May, Jun in Mumbai) pushes prices toward the top of the range as walkers cap their books.

How to save without cutting corners

  • Skip weekend and public-holiday premium slots; a Tuesday walk is materially cheaper.
  • Book weekly slots on a rolling schedule instead of one-off requests — nearly every Mumbai provider offers a package rate.
  • Avoid the Apr–Jun peak window when possible; shoulder months are cheaper and less oversubscribed.

Frequently asked questions

How much does cat sitting cost in Mumbai?

Typical 2026 rates range ₹500–₹1,180 per visit. This is a market range, not a quote — your final price depends on duration, frequency, and pet-specific needs.

Why is cat sitting in Mumbai priced the way it is?

Mumbai sits in the low cost tier for pet-care services. Local wages, insurance costs, vehicle costs (for pet-taxi), and demand during Apr, May, Jun all feed into the range.

Are weekly packages cheaper in Mumbai?

Almost always — booking a recurring weekly slot with the same cat sitter usually drops the per-visit rate by 10–20% versus a one-off booking.

Do Mumbai providers charge extra for a second pet?

Yes, most do. Per-additional-pet fees are the norm here, and they cover the real additional time and attention required — not a padding charge.

Do holiday periods change the price in Mumbai?

Yes. Public holidays and peak windows on the Mumbai calendar carry a premium, typically 20–40%, because availability tightens sharply.
