How much does dog walking cost in San Jose?

2026 typical range for San Jose, United States · Prepared by OnlyPaw · Updated 2026-09-08

Dog Walking in San Jose: $22–$35 per 30-min walk. This is the typical 2026 market range for the high cost tier that San Jose sits in. Your actual booking price depends on duration, frequency, and pet-specific needs.

What drives dog walking prices up or down in San Jose

  • Neighborhoods with limited parking or gated access add setup time per visit, which shows up in San Jose rates.
  • Anxious, reactive, or senior pets need slower onboarding — most San Jose providers price that as an extended first visit rather than a hidden surcharge.
  • Holiday windows around San Jose's calendar (public holidays, school breaks) command a 20–40% premium as availability tightens.

How to save without cutting corners

  • Ask about small-group walks — cheaper than solo for social dogs and standard practice in San Jose.
  • Bundle two services with the same provider (a walk plus a drop-in feed) and ask about a combined rate.
  • Book weekly slots on a rolling schedule instead of one-off requests — nearly every San Jose provider offers a package rate.
