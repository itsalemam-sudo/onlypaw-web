How much does dog walking cost in Rome?

2026 typical range for Rome, Italy · Prepared by OnlyPaw · Updated 2026-09-08

What drives dog walking prices up or down in Rome

  • Holiday windows around Rome's calendar (public holidays, school breaks) command a 20–40% premium as availability tightens.
  • Peak season demand (Jun, Jul, Aug in Rome) pushes prices toward the top of the range as walkers cap their books.
  • Neighborhoods with limited parking or gated access add setup time per visit, which shows up in Rome rates.

How to save without cutting corners

  • Book weekly slots on a rolling schedule instead of one-off requests — nearly every Rome provider offers a package rate.
  • Skip weekend and public-holiday premium slots; a Tuesday walk is materially cheaper.
  • Avoid the Jun–Aug peak window when possible; shoulder months are cheaper and less oversubscribed.

Frequently asked questions

Why is dog walking in Rome priced the way it is?

Rome sits in the high cost tier for pet-care services. Local wages, insurance costs, vehicle costs (for pet-taxi), and demand during Jun, Jul, Aug all feed into the range.

Are weekly packages cheaper in Rome?

Do Rome providers charge extra for a second pet?

Yes, most do. Per-additional-pet fees are the norm here, and they cover the real additional time and attention required — not a padding charge.

Do holiday periods change the price in Rome?

Yes. Public holidays and peak windows on the Rome calendar carry a premium, typically 20–40%, because availability tightens sharply.
