How much does cat sitting cost in Jakarta?

2026 typical range for Jakarta, Indonesia · Prepared by OnlyPaw · Updated 2026-09-08

Cat Sitting in Jakarta: Rp94,800–Rp221,200 per visit. This is the typical 2026 market range for the low cost tier that Jakarta sits in. Your actual booking price depends on duration, frequency, and pet-specific needs.

What drives cat sitting prices up or down in Jakarta

  • Anxious, reactive, or senior pets need slower onboarding — most Jakarta providers price that as an extended first visit rather than a hidden surcharge.
  • Holiday windows around Jakarta's calendar (public holidays, school breaks) command a 20–40% premium as availability tightens.
  • Peak season demand (Sep, Oct, Nov in Jakarta) pushes prices toward the top of the range as walkers cap their books.

How to save without cutting corners

  • Bundle two services with the same provider (a walk plus a drop-in feed) and ask about a combined rate.
  • Skip weekend and public-holiday premium slots; a Tuesday walk is materially cheaper.
  • Book weekly slots on a rolling schedule instead of one-off requests — nearly every Jakarta provider offers a package rate.

Frequently asked questions

How much does cat sitting cost in Jakarta?

Why is cat sitting in Jakarta priced the way it is?

Jakarta sits in the low cost tier for pet-care services. Local wages, insurance costs, vehicle costs (for pet-taxi), and demand during Sep, Oct, Nov all feed into the range.

Are weekly packages cheaper in Jakarta?

Almost always — booking a recurring weekly slot with the same cat sitter usually drops the per-visit rate by 10–20% versus a one-off booking.

Do Jakarta providers charge extra for a second pet?

Yes, most do. Per-additional-pet fees are the norm here, and they cover the real additional time and attention required — not a padding charge.

Do holiday periods change the price in Jakarta?

Yes. Public holidays and peak windows on the Jakarta calendar carry a premium, typically 20–40%, because availability tightens sharply.
