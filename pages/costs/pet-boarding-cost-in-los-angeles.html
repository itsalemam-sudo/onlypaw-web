How much does pet boarding cost in Los Angeles?

2026 typical range for Los Angeles, United States · Prepared by OnlyPaw · Updated 2026-09-08

Pet Boarding in Los Angeles: $45–$90 per night. This is the typical 2026 market range for the high cost tier that Los Angeles sits in. Your actual booking price depends on duration, frequency, and pet-specific needs.

What drives pet boarding prices up or down in Los Angeles

  • Multi-pet households — two dogs, or a dog plus a cat visit stacked in one trip — carry a per-additional-pet fee almost everywhere in Los Angeles.
  • Holiday windows around Los Angeles's calendar (public holidays, school breaks) command a 20–40% premium as availability tightens.
  • Anxious, reactive, or senior pets need slower onboarding — most Los Angeles providers price that as an extended first visit rather than a hidden surcharge.

How to save without cutting corners

  • Skip weekend and public-holiday premium slots; a Tuesday walk is materially cheaper.
  • Ask about small-group walks — cheaper than solo for social dogs and standard practice in Los Angeles.
  • Bundle two services with the same provider (a walk plus a drop-in feed) and ask about a combined rate.

Frequently asked questions

How much does pet boarding cost in Los Angeles?

Typical 2026 rates range $45–$90 per night. This is a market range, not a quote — your final price depends on duration, frequency, and pet-specific needs.

Why is pet boarding in Los Angeles priced the way it is?

Los Angeles sits in the high cost tier for pet-care services. Local wages, insurance costs, vehicle costs (for pet-taxi), and demand during Jul, Aug, Sep all feed into the range.

Are weekly packages cheaper in Los Angeles?

Almost always — booking a recurring weekly slot with the same pet boarder usually drops the per-visit rate by 10–20% versus a one-off booking.

Do Los Angeles providers charge extra for a second pet?

Yes, most do. Per-additional-pet fees are the norm here, and they cover the real additional time and attention required — not a padding charge.

Do holiday periods change the price in Los Angeles?

Yes. Public holidays and peak windows on the Los Angeles calendar carry a premium, typically 20–40%, because availability tightens sharply.
