How much does pet taxi cost in Buenos Aires?

2026 typical range for Buenos Aires, Argentina · Prepared by OnlyPaw · Updated 2026-09-08

Pet Taxi in Buenos Aires: AR$7,600–AR$19,000 per short ride. This is the typical 2026 market range for the low cost tier that Buenos Aires sits in. Your actual booking price depends on duration, frequency, and pet-specific needs.

What drives pet taxi prices up or down in Buenos Aires

  • Peak season demand (Dec, Jan, Feb in Buenos Aires) pushes prices toward the top of the range as walkers cap their books.
  • Holiday windows around Buenos Aires's calendar (public holidays, school breaks) command a 20–40% premium as availability tightens.
  • Anxious, reactive, or senior pets need slower onboarding — most Buenos Aires providers price that as an extended first visit rather than a hidden surcharge.

How to save without cutting corners

  • Avoid the Dec–Feb peak window when possible; shoulder months are cheaper and less oversubscribed.
  • Bundle two services with the same provider (a walk plus a drop-in feed) and ask about a combined rate.
  • Book weekly slots on a rolling schedule instead of one-off requests — nearly every Buenos Aires provider offers a package rate.
