How much does pet boarding cost in Amsterdam?

2026 typical range for Amsterdam, Netherlands · Prepared by OnlyPaw · Updated 2026-09-08

Pet Boarding in Amsterdam: €41.4–€83 per night. This is the typical 2026 market range for the high cost tier that Amsterdam sits in. Your actual booking price depends on duration, frequency, and pet-specific needs.

What drives pet boarding prices up or down in Amsterdam

  • Multi-pet households — two dogs, or a dog plus a cat visit stacked in one trip — carry a per-additional-pet fee almost everywhere in Amsterdam.
  • Anxious, reactive, or senior pets need slower onboarding — most Amsterdam providers price that as an extended first visit rather than a hidden surcharge.
  • Peak season demand (Jul, Aug in Amsterdam) pushes prices toward the top of the range as walkers cap their books.

How to save without cutting corners

  • Skip weekend and public-holiday premium slots; a Tuesday walk is materially cheaper.
  • Avoid the Jul–Aug peak window when possible; shoulder months are cheaper and less oversubscribed.
  • Book weekly slots on a rolling schedule instead of one-off requests — nearly every Amsterdam provider offers a package rate.

Frequently asked questions

How much does pet boarding cost in Amsterdam?

Typical 2026 rates range €41.4–€83 per night. This is a market range, not a quote — your final price depends on duration, frequency, and pet-specific needs.

Why is pet boarding in Amsterdam priced the way it is?

Amsterdam sits in the high cost tier for pet-care services. Local wages, insurance costs, vehicle costs (for pet-taxi), and demand during Jul, Aug all feed into the range.

Are weekly packages cheaper in Amsterdam?

Almost always — booking a recurring weekly slot with the same pet boarder usually drops the per-visit rate by 10–20% versus a one-off booking.

Do Amsterdam providers charge extra for a second pet?

Yes, most do. Per-additional-pet fees are the norm here, and they cover the real additional time and attention required — not a padding charge.

Do holiday periods change the price in Amsterdam?

Yes. Public holidays and peak windows on the Amsterdam calendar carry a premium, typically 20–40%, because availability tightens sharply.
