How much does cat sitting cost in São Paulo?

2026 typical range for São Paulo, Brazil · Prepared by OnlyPaw · Updated 2026-09-08

Cat Sitting in São Paulo: R$32.1–R$75 per visit. This is the typical 2026 market range for the low cost tier that São Paulo sits in. Your actual booking price depends on duration, frequency, and pet-specific needs.

What drives cat sitting prices up or down in São Paulo

  • Neighborhoods with limited parking or gated access add setup time per visit, which shows up in São Paulo rates.
  • Multi-pet households — two dogs, or a dog plus a cat visit stacked in one trip — carry a per-additional-pet fee almost everywhere in São Paulo.
  • Holiday windows around São Paulo's calendar (public holidays, school breaks) command a 20–40% premium as availability tightens.

How to save without cutting corners

  • Ask about small-group walks — cheaper than solo for social dogs and standard practice in São Paulo.
  • Book weekly slots on a rolling schedule instead of one-off requests — nearly every São Paulo provider offers a package rate.
  • Bundle two services with the same provider (a walk plus a drop-in feed) and ask about a combined rate.

Frequently asked questions

How much does cat sitting cost in São Paulo?

Typical 2026 rates range R$32.1–R$75 per visit. This is a market range, not a quote — your final price depends on duration, frequency, and pet-specific needs.

Why is cat sitting in São Paulo priced the way it is?

São Paulo sits in the low cost tier for pet-care services. Local wages, insurance costs, vehicle costs (for pet-taxi), and demand during Dec, Jan, Feb all feed into the range.

Are weekly packages cheaper in São Paulo?

Almost always — booking a recurring weekly slot with the same cat sitter usually drops the per-visit rate by 10–20% versus a one-off booking.

Do São Paulo providers charge extra for a second pet?

Yes, most do. Per-additional-pet fees are the norm here, and they cover the real additional time and attention required — not a padding charge.

Do holiday periods change the price in São Paulo?

Yes. Public holidays and peak windows on the São Paulo calendar carry a premium, typically 20–40%, because availability tightens sharply.
