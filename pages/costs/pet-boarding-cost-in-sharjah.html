How much does pet boarding cost in Sharjah?

2026 typical range for Sharjah, United Arab Emirates · Prepared by OnlyPaw · Updated 2026-09-08

Pet Boarding in Sharjah: 110 AED–220 AED per night. This is the typical 2026 market range for the mid cost tier that Sharjah sits in. Your actual booking price depends on duration, frequency, and pet-specific needs.

What drives pet boarding prices up or down in Sharjah

  • Holiday windows around Sharjah's calendar (public holidays, school breaks) command a 20–40% premium as availability tightens.
  • Anxious, reactive, or senior pets need slower onboarding — most Sharjah providers price that as an extended first visit rather than a hidden surcharge.
  • Peak season demand (May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct in Sharjah) pushes prices toward the top of the range as walkers cap their books.

How to save without cutting corners

  • Book weekly slots on a rolling schedule instead of one-off requests — nearly every Sharjah provider offers a package rate.
  • Avoid the May–Oct peak window when possible; shoulder months are cheaper and less oversubscribed.
  • Skip weekend and public-holiday premium slots; a Tuesday walk is materially cheaper.

Frequently asked questions

Why is pet boarding in Sharjah priced the way it is?

Sharjah sits in the mid cost tier for pet-care services. Local wages, insurance costs, vehicle costs (for pet-taxi), and demand during May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct all feed into the range.

Are weekly packages cheaper in Sharjah?

Almost always — booking a recurring weekly slot with the same pet boarder usually drops the per-visit rate by 10–20% versus a one-off booking.

Do Sharjah providers charge extra for a second pet?

Yes, most do. Per-additional-pet fees are the norm here, and they cover the real additional time and attention required — not a padding charge.

Do holiday periods change the price in Sharjah?

Yes. Public holidays and peak windows on the Sharjah calendar carry a premium, typically 20–40%, because availability tightens sharply.
