How much does dog walking cost in Buenos Aires?

2026 typical range for Buenos Aires, Argentina · Prepared by OnlyPaw · Updated 2026-09-08

Dog Walking in Buenos Aires: AR$5,700–AR$13,300 per 30-min walk. This is the typical 2026 market range for the low cost tier that Buenos Aires sits in. Your actual booking price depends on duration, frequency, and pet-specific needs.

What drives dog walking prices up or down in Buenos Aires

  • Peak season demand (Dec, Jan, Feb in Buenos Aires) pushes prices toward the top of the range as walkers cap their books.
  • Multi-pet households — two dogs, or a dog plus a cat visit stacked in one trip — carry a per-additional-pet fee almost everywhere in Buenos Aires.
  • Neighborhoods with limited parking or gated access add setup time per visit, which shows up in Buenos Aires rates.

How to save without cutting corners

  • Avoid the Dec–Feb peak window when possible; shoulder months are cheaper and less oversubscribed.
  • Book weekly slots on a rolling schedule instead of one-off requests — nearly every Buenos Aires provider offers a package rate.
  • Skip weekend and public-holiday premium slots; a Tuesday walk is materially cheaper.

Frequently asked questions

How much does dog walking cost in Buenos Aires?

Typical 2026 rates range AR$5,700–AR$13,300 per 30-min walk. This is a market range, not a quote — your final price depends on duration, frequency, and pet-specific needs.

Why is dog walking in Buenos Aires priced the way it is?

Buenos Aires sits in the low cost tier for pet-care services. Local wages, insurance costs, vehicle costs (for pet-taxi), and demand during Dec, Jan, Feb all feed into the range.

Are weekly packages cheaper in Buenos Aires?

Almost always — booking a recurring weekly slot with the same dog walker usually drops the per-visit rate by 10–20% versus a one-off booking.

Do Buenos Aires providers charge extra for a second pet?

Yes, most do. Per-additional-pet fees are the norm here, and they cover the real additional time and attention required — not a padding charge.

Do holiday periods change the price in Buenos Aires?

Yes. Public holidays and peak windows on the Buenos Aires calendar carry a premium, typically 20–40%, because availability tightens sharply.
