How much does dog daycare cost in Jacksonville?

2026 typical range for Jacksonville, United States · Prepared by OnlyPaw · Updated 2026-09-08

Dog Daycare in Jacksonville: $38–$65 per full day. This is the typical 2026 market range for the high cost tier that Jacksonville sits in. Your actual booking price depends on duration, frequency, and pet-specific needs.

What drives dog daycare prices up or down in Jacksonville

  • Peak season demand (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep in Jacksonville) pushes prices toward the top of the range as walkers cap their books.
  • Anxious, reactive, or senior pets need slower onboarding — most Jacksonville providers price that as an extended first visit rather than a hidden surcharge.
  • Multi-pet households — two dogs, or a dog plus a cat visit stacked in one trip — carry a per-additional-pet fee almost everywhere in Jacksonville.

How to save without cutting corners

  • Avoid the Jun–Sep peak window when possible; shoulder months are cheaper and less oversubscribed.
  • Ask about small-group walks — cheaper than solo for social dogs and standard practice in Jacksonville.
  • Skip weekend and public-holiday premium slots; a Tuesday walk is materially cheaper.

Yes, most do. Per-additional-pet fees are the norm here, and they cover the real additional time and attention required — not a padding charge.

Do holiday periods change the price in Jacksonville?

Yes. Public holidays and peak windows on the Jacksonville calendar carry a premium, typically 20–40%, because availability tightens sharply.
