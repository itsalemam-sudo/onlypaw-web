Frequently asked questions

How much does pet sitting typically cost in San Diego?

Typical 2026 rates range $25–$42 per drop-in visit. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book pet sitting in San Diego?

Given San Diego's warm-summer Mediterranean, mild year-round, the hot months (Aug, Sep, Oct) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a pet sitter on their first visit in San Diego?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A San Diego pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in San Diego?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
