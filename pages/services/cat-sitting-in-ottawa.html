What a good cat sitter in Ottawa looks like

  • Handles multi-pet households without stacking too many dogs on one leash.
  • Comfortable with your building's access rules (fobs, gates, doormen) on day one.
  • Reads paw-pad temperature by hand on hot surfaces before letting your dog step off the grass.
  • Can name and use at least two routes through Britannia Park, not just the nearest sidewalk.
  • Carries water and a collapsible bowl on every cat-sitting visit in Ottawa — non-negotiable in this climate.

Frequently asked questions

How much does cat sitting typically cost in Ottawa?

Typical 2026 rates range CA$27.2–CA$47.6 per visit. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book cat sitting in Ottawa?

Given Ottawa's humid continental, snowy winters, the hot months (Jul, Aug) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a cat sitter on their first visit in Ottawa?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Ottawa pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Ottawa?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
