Pet Sitting in Warsaw, Poland

Typical 2026 range · 71 zł–119 zł per drop-in visit · Vetted providers on OnlyPaw

Pet Sitting looks different in Warsaw than it does in a stock photo. The city's humid continental sets the rhythm — hot months (Jul, Aug) push serious walkers into the morning and late-evening slots, and cooler shoulders reopen the map. Pole Mokotowskie is on almost every regular route around here. Łazienki Park permits dogs but on-lead only, and not in the historic garden core — signs mark the boundary. That local nuance is the difference between a generic booking and one your pet actually looks forward to.

What a good pet sitter in Warsaw looks like

  • Handles multi-pet households without stacking too many dogs on one leash.
  • Reads paw-pad temperature by hand on hot surfaces before letting your dog step off the grass.
  • Can name and use at least two routes through Łazienki Park (leashed), not just the nearest sidewalk.
  • Comfortable with your building's access rules (fobs, gates, doormen) on day one.
  • Carries water and a collapsible bowl on every pet-sitting visit in Warsaw — non-negotiable in this climate.

Frequently asked questions

How much does pet sitting typically cost in Warsaw?

Typical 2026 rates range 71 zł–119 zł per drop-in visit. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book pet sitting in Warsaw?

Given Warsaw's humid continental, the hot months (Jul, Aug) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a pet sitter on their first visit in Warsaw?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Warsaw pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Warsaw?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
