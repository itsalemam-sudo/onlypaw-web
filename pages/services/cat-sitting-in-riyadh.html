Frequently asked questions

How much does cat sitting typically cost in Riyadh?

Typical 2026 rates range 75 SAR–131 SAR per visit. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book cat sitting in Riyadh?

Given Riyadh's hot desert, the hot months (May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a cat sitter on their first visit in Riyadh?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Riyadh pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Riyadh?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
