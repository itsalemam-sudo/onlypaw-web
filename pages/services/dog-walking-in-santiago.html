What a good dog walker in Santiago looks like

  • Sends real photo updates during the dog walk, not a generic ‘all done’ text at the end.
  • Handles multi-pet households without stacking too many dogs on one leash.
  • Comfortable with your building's access rules (fobs, gates, doormen) on day one.
  • Carries water and a collapsible bowl on every dog walk in Santiago — non-negotiable in this climate.
  • Knows the difference between an anxious sniff-freeze and an aggressive lockup, and reads your dog before pushing forward.

Frequently asked questions

How much does dog walking typically cost in Santiago?

Typical 2026 rates range CLP$14,250–CLP$23,750 per 30-min walk. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book dog walking in Santiago?

Given Santiago's warm-summer Mediterranean, the hot months (Dec, Jan, Feb) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a dog walker on their first visit in Santiago?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Santiago pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Santiago?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
