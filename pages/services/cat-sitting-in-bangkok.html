How much does cat sitting typically cost in Bangkok?

When are the best hours to book cat sitting in Bangkok?

Given Bangkok's tropical savanna, hot and humid, the hot months (Mar, Apr, May) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a cat sitter on their first visit in Bangkok?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Bangkok pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Bangkok?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
