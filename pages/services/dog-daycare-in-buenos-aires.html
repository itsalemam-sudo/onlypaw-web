Dog Daycare in Buenos Aires, Argentina

Typical 2026 range · AR$9,500–AR$20,900 per full day · Vetted providers on OnlyPaw

Dog Daycare looks different in Buenos Aires than it does in a stock photo. The city's humid subtropical sets the rhythm — hot months (Dec, Jan, Feb) push serious walkers into the morning and late-evening slots, and cooler shoulders reopen the map. Parque Centenario is on almost every regular route around here. Group walks (paseadores with 6–10 dogs) are a well-established local trade — solo walks are the premium tier. That local nuance is the difference between a generic booking and one your pet actually looks forward to.

What a good daycare host in Buenos Aires looks like

  • Handles multi-pet households without stacking too many dogs on one leash.
  • Carries water and a collapsible bowl on every daycare day in Buenos Aires — non-negotiable in this climate.
  • Comfortable with your building's access rules (fobs, gates, doormen) on day one.
  • Can name and use at least two routes through Parque Centenario, not just the nearest sidewalk.
  • Reads paw-pad temperature by hand on hot surfaces before letting your dog step off the grass.

Frequently asked questions

How much does dog daycare typically cost in Buenos Aires?

Typical 2026 rates range AR$9,500–AR$20,900 per full day. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book dog daycare in Buenos Aires?

Given Buenos Aires's humid subtropical, the hot months (Dec, Jan, Feb) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a daycare host on their first visit in Buenos Aires?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Buenos Aires pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Buenos Aires?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
