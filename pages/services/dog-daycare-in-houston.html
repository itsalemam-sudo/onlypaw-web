Frequently asked questions

How much does dog daycare typically cost in Houston?

Typical 2026 rates range $38–$65 per full day. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book dog daycare in Houston?

Given Houston's humid subtropical with hot muggy summers, the hot months (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a daycare host on their first visit in Houston?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Houston pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Houston?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
