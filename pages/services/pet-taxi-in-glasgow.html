When are the best hours to book pet taxi in Glasgow?

Given Glasgow's temperate oceanic, mild and wet, the hot months (Jul, Aug) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a pet-taxi driver on their first visit in Glasgow?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Glasgow pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.
