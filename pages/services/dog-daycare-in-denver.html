Frequently asked questions

How much does dog daycare typically cost in Denver?

Typical 2026 rates range $38–$65 per full day. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book dog daycare in Denver?

Given Denver's semi-arid with sunny cold winters and warm dry summers, the hot months (Jun, Jul, Aug) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a daycare host on their first visit in Denver?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Denver pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Denver?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
