Cat Sitting in Dubai, United Arab Emirates

Typical 2026 range · 73 AED–128 AED per visit · Vetted providers on OnlyPaw

Every city has its own version of cat sitting, and Dubai's version bends around one fact: hot desert, extreme summer heat. From May onward, the walk window narrows. Locals who care about their dog's pads know Zabeel Park and cycle two or three routes through it depending on wind and time. Dubai Municipality requires dogs on-lead in most public parks, and some parks don't permit dogs at all — the Municipality's park list is worth checking before a first visit. Ask any prospective cat sitter how they'd handle a peak-heat day and you'll learn most of what you need to know in two sentences.

What a good cat sitter in Dubai looks like

  • Knows the nearest 24-hour emergency vet from your pickup point.
  • Can name and use at least two routes through Zabeel Park, not just the nearest sidewalk.
  • Handles multi-pet households without stacking too many dogs on one leash.
  • Carries water and a collapsible bowl on every cat-sitting visit in Dubai — non-negotiable in this climate.
  • Comfortable with your building's access rules (fobs, gates, doormen) on day one.

Frequently asked questions

How much does cat sitting typically cost in Dubai?

Typical 2026 rates range 73 AED–128 AED per visit. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book cat sitting in Dubai?

Given Dubai's hot desert, extreme summer heat, the hot months (May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a cat sitter on their first visit in Dubai?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Dubai pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Dubai?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
