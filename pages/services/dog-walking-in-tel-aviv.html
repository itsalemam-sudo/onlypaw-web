What a good dog walker in Tel Aviv looks like

Frequently asked questions

How much does dog walking typically cost in Tel Aviv?

Typical 2026 rates range ₪81–₪130 per 30-min walk. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book dog walking in Tel Aviv?

Given Tel Aviv's hot-summer Mediterranean, the hot months (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a dog walker on their first visit in Tel Aviv?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Tel Aviv pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Tel Aviv?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
