What a good pet sitter in Sydney looks like

Frequently asked questions

How much does pet sitting typically cost in Sydney?

Typical 2026 rates range A$38–A$64 per drop-in visit. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book pet sitting in Sydney?

Given Sydney's humid subtropical, warm summers and mild winters, the hot months (Dec, Jan, Feb) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a pet sitter on their first visit in Sydney?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Sydney pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Sydney?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
