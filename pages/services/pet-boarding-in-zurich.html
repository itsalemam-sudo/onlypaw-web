What a good pet boarder in Zurich looks like

Frequently asked questions

How much does pet boarding typically cost in Zurich?

Typical 2026 rates range 39.6 CHF–79 CHF per night. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book pet boarding in Zurich?

Given Zurich's temperate continental with alpine influence, the hot months (Jul, Aug) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a pet boarder on their first visit in Zurich?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Zurich pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Zurich?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
