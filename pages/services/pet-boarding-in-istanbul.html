Pet Boarding in Istanbul, Türkiye

Typical 2026 range · ₺414–₺1,040 per night · Vetted providers on OnlyPaw

Every city has its own version of pet boarding, and Istanbul's version bends around one fact: transitional between Mediterranean and humid subtropical. From Jul onward, the walk window narrows. Locals who care about their dog's pads know Emirgan Park and cycle two or three routes through it depending on wind and time. Free-roaming community dogs are a fixture of city life — a walker who's confident with polite dog-dog greetings is a must here. Ask any prospective pet boarder how they'd handle a peak-heat day and you'll learn most of what you need to know in two sentences.

What a good pet boarder in Istanbul looks like

  • Knows the nearest 24-hour emergency vet from your pickup point.
  • Handles multi-pet households without stacking too many dogs on one leash.
  • Can name and use at least two routes through Emirgan Park, not just the nearest sidewalk.
  • Carries water and a collapsible bowl on every boarding night in Istanbul — non-negotiable in this climate.
  • Reads paw-pad temperature by hand on hot surfaces before letting your dog step off the grass.

Frequently asked questions

How much does pet boarding typically cost in Istanbul?

Typical 2026 rates range ₺414–₺1,040 per night. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book pet boarding in Istanbul?

Given Istanbul's transitional between Mediterranean and humid subtropical, the hot months (Jul, Aug) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a pet boarder on their first visit in Istanbul?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Istanbul pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Istanbul?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
