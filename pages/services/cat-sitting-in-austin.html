Frequently asked questions

How much does cat sitting typically cost in Austin?

Typical 2026 rates range $20–$35 per visit. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book cat sitting in Austin?

Given Austin's humid subtropical with hot summers, the hot months (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a cat sitter on their first visit in Austin?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Austin pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Austin?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
