What a good daycare host in São Paulo looks like

  • Reads paw-pad temperature by hand on hot surfaces before letting your dog step off the grass.

Frequently asked questions

How much does dog daycare typically cost in São Paulo?

Typical 2026 rates range R$54–R$118 per full day. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book dog daycare in São Paulo?

Given São Paulo's humid subtropical, the hot months (Dec, Jan, Feb) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a daycare host on their first visit in São Paulo?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A São Paulo pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in São Paulo?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.

Ibirapuera has designated dog zones — outside them, on-lead is the rule — how does that affect my booking?

A São Paulo provider working on OnlyPaw is briefed on this. Bring it up before the first visit so you're both aligned on route choice and timing.
