What a good daycare host in Tokyo looks like

  • Knows the nearest 24-hour emergency vet from your pickup point.

Frequently asked questions

How much does dog daycare typically cost in Tokyo?

Typical 2026 rates range ¥5,620–¥9,620 per full day. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book dog daycare in Tokyo?

Given Tokyo's humid subtropical, the hot months (Jul, Aug, Sep) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a daycare host on their first visit in Tokyo?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Tokyo pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Tokyo?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
