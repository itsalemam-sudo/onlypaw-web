Pet Sitting in Jakarta, Indonesia

Typical 2026 range · Rp110,600–Rp252,800 per drop-in visit · Vetted providers on OnlyPaw

Pet Sitting looks different in Jakarta than it does in a stock photo. The city's tropical monsoon sets the rhythm — hot months (Sep, Oct, Nov) push serious walkers into the morning and late-evening slots, and cooler shoulders reopen the map. Taman Suropati is on almost every regular route around here. Traffic and heat make short frequent walks the norm — 20-minute loops beat one long midday outing. That local nuance is the difference between a generic booking and one your pet actually looks forward to.

What a good pet sitter in Jakarta looks like

  • Adjusts schedule around Jakarta's hot months (Sep, Oct, Nov) rather than forcing a fixed slot.
  • Handles multi-pet households without stacking too many dogs on one leash.
  • Comfortable with your building's access rules (fobs, gates, doormen) on day one.
  • Reads paw-pad temperature by hand on hot surfaces before letting your dog step off the grass.
  • Can name and use at least two routes through Taman Suropati, not just the nearest sidewalk.

Frequently asked questions

How much does pet sitting typically cost in Jakarta?

Typical 2026 rates range Rp110,600–Rp252,800 per drop-in visit. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book pet sitting in Jakarta?

Given Jakarta's tropical monsoon, the hot months (Sep, Oct, Nov) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a pet sitter on their first visit in Jakarta?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Jakarta pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Jakarta?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
