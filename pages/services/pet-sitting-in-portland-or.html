How much does pet sitting typically cost in Portland?

When are the best hours to book pet sitting in Portland?

Given Portland's oceanic, wet cool winters and dry warm summers, the hot months (Jul, Aug) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a pet sitter on their first visit in Portland?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Portland pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.
