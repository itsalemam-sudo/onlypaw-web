Dog Walking in Kuwait City, Kuwait

Typical 2026 range · 6.8 KWD–10.9 KWD per 30-min walk · Vetted providers on OnlyPaw

Dog Walking looks different in Kuwait City than it does in a stock photo. The city's hot desert sets the rhythm — hot months (May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) push serious walkers into the morning and late-evening slots, and cooler shoulders reopen the map. Green Island is on almost every regular route around here. Summer highs above 45°C — outdoor walking is a dawn/dusk activity from May to September. That local nuance is the difference between a generic booking and one your pet actually looks forward to.

What a good dog walker in Kuwait City looks like

  • Handles multi-pet households without stacking too many dogs on one leash.
  • Reads paw-pad temperature by hand on hot surfaces before letting your dog step off the grass.
  • Carries water and a collapsible bowl on every dog walk in Kuwait City — non-negotiable in this climate.
  • Can name and use at least two routes through Green Island, not just the nearest sidewalk.
  • Comfortable with your building's access rules (fobs, gates, doormen) on day one.

Frequently asked questions

How much does dog walking typically cost in Kuwait City?

Typical 2026 rates range 6.8 KWD–10.9 KWD per 30-min walk. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book dog walking in Kuwait City?

Given Kuwait City's hot desert, the hot months (May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a dog walker on their first visit in Kuwait City?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Kuwait City pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Kuwait City?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
