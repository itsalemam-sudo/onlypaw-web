What a good pet sitter in Melbourne looks like

  • Handles multi-pet households without stacking too many dogs on one leash.
  • Comfortable with your building's access rules (fobs, gates, doormen) on day one.
  • Can name and use at least two routes through Royal Park, not just the nearest sidewalk.
  • Reads paw-pad temperature by hand on hot surfaces before letting your dog step off the grass.
  • Carries water and a collapsible bowl on every pet-sitting visit in Melbourne — non-negotiable in this climate.

Frequently asked questions

How much does pet sitting typically cost in Melbourne?

Typical 2026 rates range A$38–A$64 per drop-in visit. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book pet sitting in Melbourne?

Given Melbourne's temperate oceanic, cool winters and warm summers, the hot months (Dec, Jan, Feb) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a pet sitter on their first visit in Melbourne?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Melbourne pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Melbourne?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
