Dog Walking in Singapore, Singapore

Typical 2026 range · S$29.5–S$46.9 per 30-min walk · Vetted providers on OnlyPaw

Every city has its own version of dog walking, and Singapore's version bends around one fact: tropical rainforest, hot and humid year-round. From Jan onward, the walk window narrows. Locals who care about their dog's pads know East Coast Park and cycle two or three routes through it depending on wind and time. NParks maintains designated Dogs-Off-Leash Areas — outside them, leashes are mandatory. Ask any prospective dog walker how they'd handle a peak-heat day and you'll learn most of what you need to know in two sentences.

What a good dog walker in Singapore looks like

  • Knows the nearest 24-hour emergency vet from your pickup point.
  • Handles multi-pet households without stacking too many dogs on one leash.
  • Reads paw-pad temperature by hand on hot surfaces before letting your dog step off the grass.
  • Comfortable with your building's access rules (fobs, gates, doormen) on day one.
  • Can name and use at least two routes through Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park, not just the nearest sidewalk.

Frequently asked questions

How much does dog walking typically cost in Singapore?

Typical 2026 rates range S$29.5–S$46.9 per 30-min walk. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book dog walking in Singapore?

Given Singapore's tropical rainforest, hot and humid year-round, the hot months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a dog walker on their first visit in Singapore?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Singapore pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Singapore?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
