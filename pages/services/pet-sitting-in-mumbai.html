What a good pet sitter in Mumbai looks like

  • Reads paw-pad temperature by hand on hot surfaces before letting your dog step off the grass.
  • Can name and use at least two routes through Bandra Bandstand promenade, not just the nearest sidewalk.
  • Carries water and a collapsible bowl on every pet-sitting visit in Mumbai — non-negotiable in this climate.
  • Comfortable with your building's access rules (fobs, gates, doormen) on day one.
  • Handles multi-pet households without stacking too many dogs on one leash.

Frequently asked questions

How much does pet sitting typically cost in Mumbai?

Typical 2026 rates range ₹590–₹1,340 per drop-in visit. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book pet sitting in Mumbai?

Given Mumbai's tropical monsoon, the hot months (Apr, May, Jun) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a pet sitter on their first visit in Mumbai?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Mumbai pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Mumbai?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.

Street-dog interactions are common — a walker who reads dog-dog body language is far more valuable than one who doesn't — how does that affect my booking?

A Mumbai provider working on OnlyPaw is briefed on this. Bring it up before the first visit so you're both aligned on route choice and timing.
