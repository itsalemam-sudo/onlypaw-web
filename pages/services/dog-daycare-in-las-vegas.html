Dog Daycare in Las Vegas, United States

Typical 2026 range · $38–$65 per full day · Vetted providers on OnlyPaw

Every city has its own version of dog daycare, and Las Vegas's version bends around one fact: hot desert with cool winters. From May onward, the walk window narrows. Locals who care about their dog's pads know Barkin' Basin Park and cycle two or three routes through it depending on wind and time. Summer walking is a pre-dawn or post-9pm sport — asphalt stays hot well past sunset in July. Ask any prospective daycare host how they'd handle a peak-heat day and you'll learn most of what you need to know in two sentences.

Frequently asked questions

How much does dog daycare typically cost in Las Vegas?

Typical 2026 rates range $38–$65 per full day. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book dog daycare in Las Vegas?

Given Las Vegas's hot desert with cool winters, the hot months (May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a daycare host on their first visit in Las Vegas?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Las Vegas pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Las Vegas?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
