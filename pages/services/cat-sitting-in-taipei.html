Cat Sitting looks different in Taipei than it does in a stock photo. The city's humid subtropical sets the rhythm — hot months (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) push serious walkers into the morning and late-evening slots, and cooler shoulders reopen the map. Xinsheng Park is on almost every regular route around here. Da'an Forest Park has a fenced dog area — off-lead outside it is not permitted. What a good cat sitter in Taipei looks like

  • Handles multi-pet households without stacking too many dogs on one leash.
  • Can name and use at least two routes through Bihu Park, not just the nearest sidewalk.
  • Reads paw-pad temperature by hand on hot surfaces before letting your dog step off the grass.
  • Carries water and a collapsible bowl on every cat-sitting visit in Taipei — non-negotiable in this climate.
  • Comfortable with your building's access rules (fobs, gates, doormen) on day one.

Frequently asked questions

How much does cat sitting typically cost in Taipei?

Typical 2026 rates range NT$448–NT$800 per visit. Ranges reflect duration, day of week, and how the provider structures their packages.

When are the best hours to book cat sitting in Taipei?

Given Taipei's humid subtropical, the hot months (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a cat sitter on their first visit in Taipei?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Taipei pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Taipei?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.

Da'an Forest Park has a fenced dog area — off-lead outside it is not permitted — how does that affect my booking?

A Taipei provider working on OnlyPaw is briefed on this. Bring it up before the first visit so you're both aligned on route choice and timing.
