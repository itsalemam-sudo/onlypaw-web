When are the best hours to book dog walking in Halifax?

Given Halifax's humid continental with maritime influence, the hot months (Jul, Aug) push scheduled visits into cooler pockets — early morning and after sunset are the standard. Shoulder months open the day up.

What should I ask a dog walker on their first visit in Halifax?

Ask which routes they use in your neighborhood, how they read heat, cold, or weather stress in a dog, and how they'd handle an emergency. A Halifax pro can name three routes and one nearest 24-hour vet without hesitation.

Are group walks common in Halifax?

Yes — small-group walks are widely available, priced below solo, and work well for social dogs. If your dog is reactive or recovering from injury, book solo and say why upfront.
