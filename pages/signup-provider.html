Become a provider

Apply in 2 minutes. Complete document verification in the app.

Fill in your basics here. We'll create your account and email you a link to download the app, where you finish verification by uploading your ID, a selfie, and (for boarding) home photos.

What happens next

  1. You'll get a verification email immediately. Confirm it to activate your account.
  2. Download the OnlyPaw app and sign in with the email and password you just set.
  3. In the app, upload your government ID, a selfie, and (for boarding) 3-5 photos of your home.
  4. Complete the short knowledge test for each service you're offering.
  5. Our team reviews your application within 1-2 business days. You'll get a push notification when you're approved.
